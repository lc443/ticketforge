Load Testing Lab

Real traffic, measured thresholds, and the first evidence-backed TicketForge bottleneck.

The problem

You don't know where it breaks until you push it.

Caching, scaling, and rate limiting are assumptions unless traffic measures where the system actually stops serving users.

The solution

Generate load, define thresholds, and find the wall.

Run reproducible k6 scenarios against NGINX. Track business success, p95/p99 latency, rate-limit rejections, and correctness.

Why it works
  • Finds the real bottleneck instead of the assumed one.
  • Separates HTTP speed from business success — a fast 429 still failed the user.
  • Creates a baseline for later architecture comparisons.
