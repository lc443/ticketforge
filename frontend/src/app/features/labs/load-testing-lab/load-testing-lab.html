Load Testing Lab

TicketForge has never actually been load-tested. Here's what that would show.

The problem

You don't know where it breaks until you push it.

Every optimization sprint after this one — caching, rate limiting, scaling — is a guess about the bottleneck unless something actually measured where it is first.

The solution

Generate load, watch it degrade, find the wall.

10 → 100 → 1,000 → 5,000 concurrent users. Track requests/sec, p50/p95/p99 latency, and error rate at each level, not just "does it work."

Why it works
  • Finds the real bottleneck instead of the assumed one.
  • p95/p99, not average — the number that matches what a real user feels.
  • A baseline to compare against once Sprint 4–6 fixes land.
