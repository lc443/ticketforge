Solutions architect practice

The loop used in every sprint

01

Discover

Clarify users, constraints, risks, and quality attributes.

02

Decide

Compare options and document why one fits the context.

03

Build

Implement the smallest system change that tests the decision.

04

Prove

Use tests, metrics, failure drills, and operational evidence.

05

Defend

Explain the design, tradeoffs, cost, security, and next risks.
